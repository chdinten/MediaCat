How to copy the Discogs sales table
  1. Open the Discogs sales-history page in your browser and log in if prompted.
  2. Find the sales table. It looks like this:
DateConditionSleeveShips FromPrice
May 14, 2026Very Good Plus (VG+)Very Good Plus (VG+)Germany€ 24.00
May 10, 2026Near Mint (NM or M-)Very Good Plus (VG+)France€ 32.50
Apr 28, 2026Very Good (VG)Good Plus (G+)Netherlands€ 16.00
  1. Click just before the first date in the top-left of the table.
  2. Hold Shift and click after the last price in the bottom-right — or drag across all rows.
  3. Press Ctrl+C (Windows / Linux) or ⌘C (Mac) to copy.
  4. Click in the Paste sales table here field above and press Ctrl+V / ⌘V.
  5. Click Preview import to check the result before saving.

MediaCat only reads what you paste here. It never accesses your Discogs session, credentials, or browser.
